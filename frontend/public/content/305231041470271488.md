# French Adjective Placement: Before or After the Noun?

In French, most adjectives **follow the noun** they modify — *un livre intéressant*, *une idée brillante*. This is the default and the safest rule to start with. However, a small but frequently used group of adjectives **precede** the noun, and knowing them is essential for natural-sounding French.

## The Default: Adjective After Noun

Colour, shape, nationality, religion, and most descriptive adjectives come after:

- *une voiture **rouge***
- *un étudiant **japonais***
- *une idée **brillante***
- *un homme **grand*** (a tall man)

## BANGS: Adjectives That Come Before

A helpful acronym — **BANGS** — covers the most common pre-noun adjectives:

| Letter | Category | Examples |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **B** | Beauty | *beau, joli* |
| **A** | Age | *jeune, vieux, nouveau, ancien* |
| **N** | Number | *premier, dernier, deuxième* |
| **G** | Goodness | *bon, mauvais, meilleur, gentil* |
| **S** | Size | *grand, petit, gros, long, court, haut* |

Examples in context:
- *un **beau** jardin* — a beautiful garden
- *une **vieille** maison* — an old house
- *le **premier** jour* — the first day
- *un **bon** repas* — a good meal

## Adjectives That Change Meaning by Position

Some adjectives carry **different meanings** depending on whether they precede or follow the noun:

| Adjective | Before noun | After noun |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| *grand* | *un **grand** homme* — a great man | *un homme **grand*** — a tall man |
| *propre* | *mon **propre** appartement* — my own apartment | *un appartement **propre*** — a clean apartment |
| *ancien* | *mon **ancienne** école* — my former school | *un bâtiment **ancien*** — an old building |
| *cher* | *ma **chère** amie* — my dear friend | *une robe **chère*** — an expensive dress |
| *même* | *la **même** chose* — the same thing | *la vérité **même*** — the very truth |
| *seul* | *le **seul** problème* — the only problem | *un homme **seul*** — a lonely man |
| *pauvre* | *le **pauvre** homme* — the poor (pitiful) man | *un pays **pauvre*** — a poor (impoverished) country |

## Agreement Rules

All French adjectives agree in **gender and number** with the noun they modify:

- Masculine singular: *un livre **intéressant***
- Feminine singular: *une idée **intéressante***
- Masculine plural: *des livres **intéressants***
- Feminine plural: *des idées **intéressantes***

When in doubt about placement, **postposition is always the safer guess** — and your meaning will still come through.

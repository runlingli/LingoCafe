French uses articles more extensively than English, and the choice of article carries precise meaning. There are three systems: **definite**, **indefinite**, and **partitive**.

## Definite Articles: le, la, l', les

Definite articles refer to something specific, or to a noun in its **general sense**.

| | Masculine | Feminine | Before vowel/h | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Form** | *le* | *la* | *l'* | *les* |
| **Example** | *le livre* | *la table* | *l'ami* | *les enfants* |

The key insight: French uses the definite article where English uses *none at all* for general statements.

- *J'aime **le** café.* — I like coffee. (coffee in general)
- ***Le** français est difficile.* — French is difficult. (the language as a concept)
- *Elle déteste **les** araignées.* — She hates spiders. (spiders in general)

## Indefinite Articles: un, une, des

Indefinite articles refer to **unspecified** items.

| | Masculine | Feminine | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form** | *un* | *une* | *des* |
| **Example** | *un café* | *une idée* | *des livres* |

**Two tricky rules:**

1. After **negation**, change to *de* (or *d'*): *J'ai des amis → Je n'ai **pas d'**amis.*
2. Before an **adjective that precedes the noun**, *des* → *de*: *de bons livres*, *de jolies fleurs*.

## Partitive Articles: du, de la, de l'

Partitive articles express **an unspecified amount** of something uncountable.

| | Masculine | Feminine | Before vowel/h |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form** | *du* | *de la* | *de l'* |
| **Example** | *du pain* | *de la chance* | *de l'eau* |

- *Je veux **du** pain.* — I want (some) bread.
- *Elle a **de la** patience.* — She has patience.
- *Bois **de l'**eau.* — Drink (some) water.

After expressions of **quantity**, all articles reduce to *de*:

> *beaucoup **de** pain, un peu **de** lait, une bouteille **d'**eau, assez **de** temps*

## Quick Reference

| Context | Article used |
|---------|-------------|
| Specific or general noun | *le / la / les* |
| One unspecified item | *un / une* |
| Unspecified plural items | *des* |
| Unspecified quantity (uncountable) | *du / de la / de l'* |
| After negation | *de / d'* |
| After quantity expression | *de / d'* |

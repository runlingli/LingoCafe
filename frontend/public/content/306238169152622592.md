There are three ways to ask a yes/no question in French. All three are grammatically correct — the choice is about **register**.

## Method 1: Rising Intonation

Simply raise your pitch at the end of a statement. No word order change needed.

> *Tu parles français?*
> *Elle est déjà partie?*

**Register**: Informal / spoken only. How friends talk. Perfectly correct in conversation, never used in formal writing.

## Method 2: Est-ce que

Place *est-ce que* (or *est-ce qu'* before a vowel) before a normal statement.

> *Est-ce que tu parles français?*
> *Est-ce qu'il comprend?*

**Register**: Neutral — correct in both speech and writing, formal and informal. **When in doubt, use this.**

## Method 3: Subject-Verb Inversion

Reverse the subject pronoun and the verb, joined by a hyphen.

> *Parlez-vous français?*
> *A-t-elle compris?*

**Register**: Formal. Used in writing, official speech, and polite address.

### The -t- insertion rule

When a third-person singular verb ends in a vowel, a **-t-** is inserted between the verb and the pronoun for pronunciation:

> *Mange**-t-**il? / A**-t-**elle compris? / Va**-t-**on y aller?*

### With noun subjects

Inversion uses a pronoun that copies the noun:

> *Marie parle**-t-elle** français?* (not *Parle Marie?*)

## Information Questions

For *who, what, where, when, why, how*, combine a question word with any of the three methods:

| Question word | Est-ce que form | Inversion form |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| *où* (where) | *Où est-ce que tu habites?* | *Où habites-tu?* |
| *quand* (when) | *Quand est-ce qu'il arrive?* | *Quand arrive-t-il?* |
| *pourquoi* (why) | *Pourquoi est-ce que tu pars?* | *Pourquoi pars-tu?* |
| *comment* (how) | *Comment est-ce qu'on fait?* | *Comment fait-on?* |
| *combien* (how much) | *Combien est-ce que ça coûte?* | *Combien cela coûte-t-il?* |

**Que / Qu'est-ce que** (what) deserves special attention:

- *Que fais-tu?* — inversion form (formal)
- *Qu'est-ce que tu fais?* — est-ce que form (neutral)
- *Tu fais quoi?* — intonation form (informal)

All three mean exactly the same thing: *What are you doing?*

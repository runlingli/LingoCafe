# French Connectors: Linking Ideas Like a Native Speaker

Stringing thoughts together fluently is what separates a textbook learner from someone who sounds natural. **Connectors** — also called discourse markers or linking words — are the glue that holds sentences and paragraphs together, signalling the logical relationship between ideas.

## Addition

Use these to add information:

| Connector | Meaning | Register |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| *et* | and | neutral |
| *aussi* | also | neutral |
| *de plus* | furthermore | formal |
| *en outre* | moreover | formal |
| *par ailleurs* | besides / on the other hand | formal |
| *également* | likewise, also | neutral |

> *Ce restaurant est excellent. **De plus**, les prix sont raisonnables.*

## Contrast and Opposition

Use these to show a different or opposing point:

| Connector | Meaning | Register |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| *mais* | but | neutral |
| *cependant* | however | formal |
| *pourtant* | yet, even so | neutral/formal |
| *néanmoins* | nevertheless | formal |
| *en revanche* | on the other hand | neutral/formal |
| *toutefois* | however | formal |
| *or* | now, yet (logical) | formal |

> *Je comprends ton point de vue. **Néanmoins**, je ne suis pas d'accord.*

## Cause and Consequence

| Connector | Meaning | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| *parce que* | because | Cannot start a sentence in formal writing |
| *car* | for, because | More formal; cannot start a sentence |
| *puisque* | since, given that | Implies reason is already known |
| *donc* | so, therefore | Very common |
| *par conséquent* | as a result | Formal |
| *ainsi* | thus, so | Formal |
| *c'est pourquoi* | that is why | Neutral |

> *Il pleuvait, **donc** nous sommes restés à la maison.*

## Sequencing

Use these to structure steps or narrative events:

- **D'abord** / **Premièrement** — first of all
- **Ensuite** / **Puis** — then, next
- **Après** — after, afterwards
- **Enfin** / **Finalement** — finally, lastly

> ***D'abord**, il faut mélanger les ingrédients. **Ensuite**, on laisse reposer la pâte. **Enfin**, on fait cuire au four.*

## Clarification and Example

| Connector | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| *c'est-à-dire* | that is to say |
| *autrement dit* | in other words |
| *par exemple* | for example |
| *notamment* | in particular, especially |
| *en effet* | indeed (confirms what preceded) |

> *Il maîtrise plusieurs langues, **notamment** le français et le mandarin.*

## How to Learn Them

The best way to internalise these connectors is **not** to memorise lists — it is to notice them in authentic French text. Read French opinion pieces and quality journalism (Le Monde, Le Point, Libération). Journalists use these connectors constantly, and exposure builds intuition faster than drilling.

The subjunctive is the mood that most frightens intermediate learners. The truth: the **form** is learned once; the real skill is knowing which expressions **trigger** it.

## When to Use the Subjunctive

The subjunctive appears in a dependent clause introduced by *que*, after a main clause expressing:

| Category | Triggers |
|----------|---------|
| **Wish / Will** | *vouloir que, préférer que, souhaiter que, exiger que* |
| **Necessity** | *il faut que, il est nécessaire que* |
| **Emotion** | *être content que, regretter que, avoir peur que* |
| **Doubt / Denial** | *douter que, nier que, ne pas croire que* |
| **Conjunctions** | *bien que, pour que, avant que, à moins que, quoique* |

The formula is nearly always: **[trigger] + que + [subject] + [subjunctive verb]**

> *Je veux **que** tu **viennes**.* — I want you to come.
> *Il faut **que** nous **partions**.* — We need to leave.
> *Bien **qu'**elle **soit** fatiguée, elle travaille.* — Although she is tired, she works.

## Forming the Present Subjunctive

**Step 1**: Take the *ils/elles* form of the present indicative.
**Step 2**: Drop *-ent*.
**Step 3**: Add the endings: *-e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent*.

| Infinitive | ils form | Stem | que je… |
|------------|----------|------|---------|
| *parler* | *parlent* | *parl-* | *parle* |
| *finir* | *finissent* | *finiss-* | *finisse* |
| *prendre* | *prennent* | *prenn-* | *prenne* |
| *écrire* | *écrivent* | *écriv-* | *écrive* |

## Irregular Subjunctive Stems

These must be memorised:

| Verb | Subjunctive stem | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| *être* | *soi-* | *que je sois, que tu sois, qu'il soit* |
| *avoir* | *ai-* | *que j'aie, que tu aies* |
| *aller* | *aill- / all-* | *que j'aille, que nous allions* |
| *faire* | *fass-* | *que je fasse* |
| *pouvoir* | *puiss-* | *que je puisse* |
| *savoir* | *sach-* | *que je sache* |
| *vouloir* | *veuill- / voul-* | *que je veuille, que nous voulions* |

## A Common Mistake to Avoid

The subjunctive is used only when the **subjects of the two clauses differ**. When they are the same, use an infinitive instead:

> ✓ *Je veux **partir**.* (same subject — infinitive)
> ✓ *Je veux **qu'elle parte**.* (different subject — subjunctive)
> ✗ ~~*Je veux que je parte.*~~

Mastering the subjunctive is largely a matter of memorising the trigger list and practising the irregular forms until they feel automatic.
